This is a practice exam that uses your webcam to follow where you're looking. It works best with good lighting and a quick calibration — nothing fancy, just a few clicks.

Chrome or Edge on a laptop with a built-in camera is your best bet.

First time? Read the setup guide: SETUP.md

A few things before we start

Eye tracking works a lot better when the setup is right. Take a quick look at this list:

  • Lighting Your face should be lit from the front. Try to avoid sitting with a bright window behind you.
  • Distance Sit about an arm's length from the screen (~50–70 cm) and try to keep that distance.
  • Head still Move your eyes to look around — try not to move your whole head.
  • Camera Webcam at eye level works best. Glasses are fine; sunglasses won't work.
  • Full screen Maximize this window — calibration is tied to your screen size.
