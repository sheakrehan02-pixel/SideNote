This demo runs real webcam eye tracking in your browser. Accuracy depends on lighting, camera placement, and calibration — follow every step for best results.

Best browsers: Chrome or Edge on a laptop with a built-in webcam.

First time? Read the setup guide: SETUP.md

Before you calibrate

WebGazer maps your eye features to screen coordinates. These conditions matter more than any software tweak:

  • LightingFace lit evenly from the front. Avoid backlighting (window behind you).
  • DistanceSit arm's length from the screen (~50–70 cm). Keep that distance during the exam.
  • Head stillMove your eyes, not your head, when looking around the screen.
  • CameraWebcam at eye level. Glasses are OK; sunglasses are not.
  • Full screenMaximize this window. Calibration is tied to your screen size.

9-point calibration

For each point: look at the green circle, then click it. WebGazer learns where your eyes point on this screen.
